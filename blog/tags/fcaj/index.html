Review First Cloud AI Journey "Know before you join First Cloud AI Journey"

March 24, 2026 · 3 min read
Pham Trong Nghia
Administrator

Hi everyone, welcome back to Nghia's Blog! If you're pursuing a career in Computer Science, especially if you're interested in Cloud Computing and DevOps, then you definitely can't miss the AWS ecosystem. Recently, I participated in the AWS Study Group's First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program. This program is like a "roadmap" for newcomers to the world of cloud computing. Below is what I learned about the program from the first FCAJ video.

1. What is First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)?​

  • This is a community of people learning and working with AWS, where members support each other and share knowledge in a non-toxic environment.

  • Vision: Not just for certification exams, but for long-term career support, helping members become future tech leaders and inspire the next generation.

2. Six core principles of the program:​

  • Builder and Troubleshooter Spirit: Ability to independently build products to solve real-world problems and independently troubleshoot when systems encounter issues.

  • Teamwork: An extremely important skill in an era of constantly changing technology; the program encourages group registration and learning.

  • Resilience: Perseverance and mutual support to meet the ever-increasing expectations of businesses, especially with the advent of AI.

  • Hands-on and Knowledge Sharing: Qualifications must go hand in hand with practical skills. Skills are proven through practice and confidently sharing knowledge with the community.

  • Investing in Yourself: Don't hesitate to invest time and money in learning and practicing.

  • Lifelong Learning: A continuous learning journey (the program sets a 6-month target to motivate participants, but each person may have their own pace).

3. Practice and Projects:​

  • Practical exercises are designed for students to start from scratch, rather than simply following pre-written instructions, fostering a deep understanding of how to apply the knowledge in practice.

  • Graduation requirement: Complete 5 projects.

  • The projects don't need to be massive; what matters is their accuracy, solving a business problem, and that you feel proud enough to include them on your CV or LinkedIn profile.

4. Benefits of participation:​

  • Participate in events and meetups organized by FCAJ in collaboration with universities, AWS partners, and IT companies (in Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, and Da Nang).

  • Opportunities to present and share knowledge, thereby building your personal brand and potentially using it as a profile for the AWS Community Builder position.

  • Receive support from a community of over 50,000 members (primarily through WhatsApp).

5. What do I need to prepare before starting? (Checklist):​

  • A personal AWS account.

  • AWS Builder Profile account (to access more in-depth practice exercises).

  • WhatsApp account for communication and support.

  • A professional LinkedIn profile to update your learning progress and completed projects.

  • Join community channels: AWS Study Group Facebook/LinkedIn group, WordPress blog, and two YouTube channels (one theoretical, one practical).
